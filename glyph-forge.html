Finding your activated Adobe font on disk:
Windows → %APPDATA%\Adobe\CoreSync\plugins\livetype\r\
macOS → ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CoreSync/plugins/livetype/.r/
Files have scrambled names but are plain .otf. Drop them here one at a time — the loader reports the real family name so you can tell which is which. (Use only fonts your Adobe license covers; keep outputs as artwork, don't redistribute the font.)

CSS drop shadow: use filter: drop-shadow(2px 3px 4px rgba(0,0,0,.4)) — it hugs the glyph outline. Avoid box-shadow (shadows the square bounding box). Stays crisp at any scale; pairs with the currentColor trick.
